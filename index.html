My story

I am Meenakshi Mehta

My journey has never followed a straight line - and I wouldn't have it any other way.

I've been an educator, theatre facilitator, wellness practitioner, marketing leader, experience designer, celebrity manager, author, coffee coach and storyteller. Every chapter has taught me something invaluable about people, communication and resilience.

Today, I work with organisations to shape meaningful narratives, help leaders communicate with authenticity, and inspire individuals to unlock their potential through stories.

Professionally, I've spent over two decades crafting stories & shaping communications. My work has centred on creating meaningful experiences and leading creative teams for some of the world's most respected brands. Personally, surviving cancer transformed my understanding of courage, purpose, and gratitude and reinforced my belief in the power of every story.

Everything I do today sits at the intersection of strategy, storytelling and human connection.
